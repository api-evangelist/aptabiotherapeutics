Rovunaptabin was recently evaluated in a randomised, placebo-controlled phase 2 clinical study (BLOC), conducted at 15 sites across five EU countries (NCT05911009). The study enrolled 114 patients with Long COVID and compared the efficacy of two dosage levels of rovunaptabin against placebo control. The study confirmed that rovunaptabin was safe and well tolerated.
